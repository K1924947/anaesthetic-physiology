About

Who I am

I'm Dr Ed Kim, a UK Foundation Year 2 (FY2) doctor preparing for anaesthetic training. I build these tools in my spare time. They started as personal study tools; if they help others, all the better.

Why

Physiology sticks when you understand it from first principles. Changing a value and watching the loop or strip respond builds understanding that lasts, not the kind that gets you through an exam and disappears.

What it is

Free, interactive core physiology tools: cardiac now, respiratory to follow. Sources include Resus Council UK (RCUK) guidelines, the BNF and standard physiology texts. Everything runs in your browser, with no account and no download.

Who it's for

Medical students, foundation doctors and anyone revising physiology, including for exams such as the MSRA, FRCA and USMLE.

A teaching tool, not clinical guidance. Always follow local protocols and current guidelines. Spotted an error or have an idea? Use the 2 minute feedback form.
