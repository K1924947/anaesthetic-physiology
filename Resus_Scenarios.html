How scenarios work
  1. 1Pick a listed case, or choose Random scenario. Random picks from all of the cases in the list. The name of a random case is revealed in the debrief, not while you are playing.
  2. 2Read the patient and the monitor, then choose actions in the order you would in real life. Use Identify reversible causes and Ask for / check when you need a list of causes or a test result. Each opens as a pop-up, so you do not have to scroll the actions list. Requested results, and Cycle NIBP now, appear after a short real-time delay and do not advance the simulated clock. You can keep treating while they are pending. After the first Cycle NIBP, the cuff then repeats every 2 minutes of simulated time; a further Cycle NIBP now resets that interval. Nothing you should have to request is shown up front.
  3. 3CPR for 2 minutes performs two minutes of CPR and shows the rhythm on the monitor. Skip time only advances the clock and does not count as an action. Check rhythm and pulse is a separate step.
  4. 4With Show solution off, feedback still gives a short reason when an action is not quite or partly right, and a one-line reason if a dangerous error ends the case. It will not show the full model answer or tell you the next step.
  5. 5If a key step is overdue you will get escalating nudges. After the third nudge the case ends. The debrief names the missed step and the correct action; the live nudges stay vague.
  6. 6Call the reg gives a hint. Each call deducts 0.5 from the decision score, with a minimum of 0.
  7. 7Show solution is off by default. Turn it on for more guidance, including why the rhythm changed and the guideline link.
  8. 8The debrief shows your score, what you missed, and the guideline for each decision.
  9. 9An unsynchronised shock in a patient with a pulse ends the case immediately, whether Show solution is on or off, with the words Patient harmed: scenario ended and a short reason. It would fail the ALS assessment. Other dangerous errors (for example CPR in a patient with a pulse, or shocking asystole) end the case immediately when Show solution is off; with Show solution on, those are marked Not quite and the case continues. The debrief explains why.
  10. 10Restart starts the same case again.
