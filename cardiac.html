Cardiac

Five tools are live: the Cardiac Action Potential, ECG Basics, the ECG Rhythms tool, the Pressure-Volume Loop and Cardiac Murmurs. Three more tools are under construction while they are rebuilt and rechecked. Arrest and peri-arrest cases are on Resus Scenarios, from the home page.

Suggested learning order

  1. Cardiac Action Potential Ionic foundations
  2. ECG Basics Waves, intervals and rate
  3. ECG Rhythms Rhythms on the strip
  4. Pressure-Volume Loop The cardiac cycle
  5. Arterial Pressure Waveform Under construction The loop unrolled into time
  6. Cardiac Murmurs Sounds and haemodynamics
  7. Heart Failure Under construction Notes and mechanisms
  8. Myocardial Infarction Timeline Under construction Pathology over time
