Privacy notice

This notice covers the Plus waitlist only: the email address you give us so we can tell you when Physio Lab Plus is available, and related updates about that launch. It does not cover paid accounts, payments, or a general site analytics programme. If those are added later, this notice must be replaced or extended before they collect any new personal data.

Last updated: 27 November 2026.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Edward Kim, who runs The Physio Lab as a sole operator.

Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

Website: https://thephysiolab.co.uk

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • The date and time you joined, the fact that you ticked the consent box, and the version of this notice and the consent wording that was shown.
  • Optional information you choose to give, such as whether you are a medical student or a foundation doctor, if that question is included on the form.
  • Technical data that is created when you submit the form, such as a timestamp and basic server logs that may include an IP address, only if our hosting or form tools record that in the ordinary running of the site.

We do not ask for your name, university, phone number, or date of birth for the waitlist, and you should not include patient or other sensitive information in any message you send us.

Why we use this information, and the lawful basis

We use your email address to:

  • confirm that you have joined the waitlist;
  • email you when Physio Lab Plus is ready, and with a small number of related updates about the launch.

The lawful basis is consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Sending these emails is also electronic mail marketing, so we only send them if you have given consent in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. You give that consent by ticking the box on the form. The box is not pre-ticked.

We do not use waitlist emails for any other marketing, and we do not sell or share them with anyone for their marketing.

Where the information is stored

Waitlist emails are stored in a database provided by Supabase. The project is hosted in London (eu-west-2).

Supabase and any email-delivery provider we use are processors. We will have an appropriate contract with them, including a data processing agreement where required. If a provider stores or accesses information outside the UK, we will only do that where UK data-protection law allows it, for example under an adequacy decision or a UK-approved transfer tool.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until the earlier of these:

  • you withdraw your consent or unsubscribe;
  • 24 months after we last emailed you about Plus, if you have not become a user of a paid product;
  • or the point at which the waitlist is closed and we no longer need the list, at which point we will delete unused addresses or ask you again if we have a new, specific reason to keep in touch.

We may keep a minimal suppression record (your email address and the fact that you unsubscribed) so that we do not email you again by mistake.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of anything we did before you withdrew it;
  • access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • ask us to erase your data, in the circumstances the law allows;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data, in the circumstances the law allows;
  • object to our use of your data, including an absolute right to object to use for direct marketing;
  • receive your data in a portable form, where the lawful basis is consent and the processing is carried out by automated means.

To use any of these rights, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We will also include an unsubscribe link in every waitlist email. Unsubscribing stops marketing emails and is treated as withdrawal of consent for that purpose.
