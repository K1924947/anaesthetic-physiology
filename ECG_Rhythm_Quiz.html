How the strips are made

Every strip is synthesised in the browser from simple wave components (P, QRS, T, flutter and fibrillatory baselines, pacing spikes) placed on a 10 second lead II timeline at 25 mm/s and 10 mm/mV. A seeded random generator varies the rate and intervals within the ranges given for each rhythm. These are teaching approximations, not recordings: real ECGs are noisier and more varied. Bundle branch blocks also show synthetic V1 and V6 strips because one limb lead cannot diagnose them. Conduction diagrams are schematic and not to scale.
