About this model

  • This is a simplified teaching model. At any instant many depolarisation waves spread through the heart at once; their sum is the mean electrical vector, shown here as one arrow that changes size and direction through the beat. The traces are computed from that arrow, not recorded from a patient.
  • Each lead records the arrow's component along its own line: upright when the arrow points towards the + pole, downward when it points away, and no net deflection when it is at right angles to the lead.
  • The limb leads use the hexaxial angles: I 0°, II +60°, aVF +90°, aVR -150° (positive angles point downwards). In this model the augmented leads (aVR, aVF) are drawn at 0.87 times the size of I and II.
  • In this model the chest leads lie in a horizontal plane through the heart, with V6 pointing to the patient's left and V1 forwards and slightly to the right. Their exact angles here are schematic.
  • The heart shape, arrow directions, timings and sizes are stylised and were chosen for teaching. The model beat runs at 75 per minute.
